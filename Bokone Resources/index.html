About Us

At Bokone Resources we pride ourselves in excellence across property investment, development and centre management.

Bokone Resources® (Pty) Ltd is a proudly South African property investment and development company based in Mothibistad, Kuruman, Northern Cape. Owned by Pelele Kitso and Sewedi Tuelo, the company focuses on creating high-quality commercial facilities that serve the mining, farming and residential communities of the region.


Our flagship project, Phakane Crossings, is strategically located at the N14 intersection with Tlhabane Road. The development will deliver a modern regional shopping centre with national retailers, local businesses, essential services and generous parking.


We hold ourselves to the highest standards of professional ethics and accountability.
